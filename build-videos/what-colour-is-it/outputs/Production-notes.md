No new characters were necessary. This note records continuity for this production; it does not replace or change the master character bible.

## What was made

A new edit with three complete existing school songs, gently moving character cutouts, changing toy and food illustrations, chapter titles, a lighthouse setting and chapter transitions. School artwork and recordings were reused; food props and the graphic backgrounds were created for this edit.

This is a simple animated music-video draft, not a fully articulated or lip-synchronised character animation. Object labels are visual vocabulary prompts, not timed lyric captions. Their changes are not aligned word-for-word with the vocals.

The new edit follows the broad compilation format: approachable characters, clear vocabulary, repeated musical activities and colourful scenes. It contains three songs rather than reproducing the reference's twenty-song running order.
